================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 5b: how much of a target password can the attacker cover?
================================================================================
Fraction of the target password's characters and digraphs that appear
in the victim's profile built from every OTHER password they typed.
Uncovered positions fall back to a population estimate, which is the
weak form of the attack.

A positive correlation means the attack succeeds precisely where the
victim's other passwords cover the target's digraphs, which is the
mechanism the composition result needs.
