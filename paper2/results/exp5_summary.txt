Reading
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If the ordering is preserved, the 2021/2023 policy advice still holds and
this is a robustness result. If it changes, a policy chosen to maximise
accuracy against strangers is not the policy that survives a real attacker,
which is a direct correction to that advice.
